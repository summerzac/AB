About Property Buyer Now

A straight-talking cash property buyer, built around one idea: selling your home should be simple, fast and certain.

Who we are

Property Buyer Now is a UK-based professional property buying company. We purchase houses, flats and bungalows directly from their owners using our own cash funds — which means no mortgage approvals, no chains, and no risk of your sale collapsing at the last minute.

We exist for the situations the traditional market handles badly: the inherited house three counties away, the relocation with a fixed start date, the chain that broke a week before exchange, the repossession clock that won't stop ticking. In those moments, speed and certainty are worth more than squeezing out the last pound of asking price — and that's exactly what we provide.

How we make money (full transparency)

We buy at a discount to open market value — typically we pay 80–85% — and later resell or rent the property. That discount is our margin, and it's how we can afford to pay your legal fees, absorb all the transaction risk, and complete in days. We'd rather explain that plainly than pretend otherwise: if maximising price matters more to you than speed, an estate agent is the right choice and we'll tell you so.

Our commitments

  • No fees, ever. Agent commission: £0. Legal fees: covered. Valuations: at our expense.
  • No last-minute price drops. Our formal written offer is the amount you receive on completion.
  • No pressure. Every offer is free and no-obligation, and you can walk away at any point before exchange.
  • Plain English. No jargon, no small print surprises, and honest advice even when it means recommending an estate agent instead.
