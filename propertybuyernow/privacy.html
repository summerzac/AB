1. Who we are

Property Buyer Now (“we”, “us”) operates propertybuyernow.co.uk. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website. [PLACEHOLDER: registered company name, company number, registered office, ICO registration number, and a contact email for data-protection queries.]

2. What we collect

  • Enquiry details you provide through our forms: name, phone number, email address, property address and postcode, and information about your property and selling situation.
  • Technical data strictly necessary to run the site. We use browser storage (localStorage) to remember your enquiry and your cookie-notice preference. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. How we use your information

  • To research your property and prepare your cash offer.
  • To contact you about your enquiry by phone or email, as you've requested.
  • To progress a sale you've agreed to, including sharing necessary details with solicitors and surveyors involved in your transaction.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including anti-money-laundering checks where a sale proceeds.

Our lawful bases are: taking steps at your request prior to entering a contract, our legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry, and legal obligation for compliance checks.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We never pass your details to other “quick sale” companies or lead brokers.
  • We never send marketing you haven't agreed to.

5. How long we keep your data

Enquiry data is kept for up to 24 months from your last contact with us, then deleted. If you sell to us, transaction records are kept for 6 years to meet legal and tax obligations.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or stop using your personal information, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at [PLACEHOLDER: privacy email]. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Changes to this policy
