How it works

From your first enquiry to funds in your bank, here's exactly what happens — and what it costs you (nothing).

  1. Day 1

    Tell us about your property

    Complete the 60-second online form or call us free on 0800 000 0000. We'll ask about the property, its condition and your ideal timescale. No paperwork, no photos, no viewings.

  2. Within 24 hours

    We research and make your offer

    Our team analyses recent sold prices, local demand and your property's details, then calls you with a provisional cash offer. It's free, and there's no obligation to accept.

  3. Days 2–5

    Valuation and formal offer

    If you're happy to proceed we arrange an independent valuation at a time that suits you, at our expense. We then confirm our formal written offer — the exact amount you'll receive.

  4. Days 5–7

    Solicitors instructed — at our cost

    Accept the offer and we instruct solicitors immediately. You can use your own solicitor or one from our panel; either way we cover your legal fees. There's nothing for you to pay.

  5. From day 7 — or your date

    Exchange, complete, get paid

    Because we buy with our own cash there's no mortgage lender, no chain and no waiting. We exchange and complete on the day you choose, and the money lands in your bank account the same day.
